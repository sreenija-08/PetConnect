1. Pet Adoption

Find your forever companion

Adopting through PetConnect is structured, safe, and transparent:

1
Browse Verified Pets

Search dogs and cats by breed, age, gender, and location across verified rescue shelters.

2
Submit Online Application

Fill out your home type, pet ownership experience, and daily hours alone for shelter review.

3
Shelter Visit & Interaction

Meet the pet in person, interact, and receive shelter recommendation.

4
Admin Approval & Handover

Admin verifies application authenticity, followed by official pet handover!

2. Temporary Pet Care

Safe boarding while you travel

Board your pet safely at verified shelters without transferring ownership:

1
Register Your Pet

Add your pet's dietary, medical, and vaccination details under 'My Pets'.

2
Search Available Shelters

Filter by location, check-in/out dates, daily boarding fee, and live real-time capacity.

3
Shelter Acceptance & Check-In

Shelter owner accepts your booking and confirms check-in when you drop off your pet.

4
Pick Up & Stay Completion

Pick up your healthy pet. The shelter marks the stay completed and capacity is released.
